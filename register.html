You can upgrade to another membership type whenever you’re ready.

Individual Membership

Individual Membership is for anyone who wants to become part of the BWY community and enjoy the full range of member benefits.

Membership includes:

  • Access to local BWY communities across England, Scotland and Wales.
  • Training, workshops, festivals and community events.
  • Free live online BWY Member Sessions.
  • A welcome pack and Spectrum magazine.
  • Member discounts and partner offers.
  • Opportunities to volunteer through Yoga in Action.

This membership is also suitable if you’re beginning Foundation 1 or Foundation 2 study with BWY.

Family Membership

Family Membership is for an additional member living at the same address as an existing BWY member.

You receive the same general membership benefits at a reduced price.

Family members share print benefits such as Spectrum magazine at the same address.

Student Teachers, Teachers and people following relevant training pathways need the membership appropriate to their status.

Overseas Membership

Overseas Membership is for people living outside the UK who want to remain part of the BWY community.

Membership includes:

  • Online training, workshops and community events.
  • Free live online BWY Member Sessions.
  • Spectrum magazine and member resources.
  • Member discounts and selected partner offers.
  • Access to online BWY community activity.

Student Teacher Membership

Student Teacher Membership supports you while studying on a BWY Yoga Teacher Training course.

It includes the benefits of general BWY Membership, plus professional support for your developing teaching practice.

Additional benefits include:

  • Teaching insurance.
  • Promotion of your classes, workshops and events on the BWY website.
  • Training, CPD and career development opportunities.
  • Access to teaching and CPD resources.
  • A TOTUM student card for eligible students.
  • Use of the BWY Student Teacher logo.

Teacher / Associate Teacher Membership

Teacher and Associate Teacher Membership offers professional support, recognition and resources for yoga teachers.

It includes the benefits of general BWY Membership, plus additional teaching benefits.

Additional benefits include:

  • Teaching insurance.
  • Promotion of classes, workshops, events and retreats on the BWY website.
  • Training, CPD and career development opportunities.
  • Access to teaching and CPD resources.
  • Use of the appropriate Teacher or Associate Teacher logo.

Some entitlements differ between Teacher and Associate Teacher status.

Compare

Overseas Teacher Membership

Overseas Teacher Membership is for BWY teachers living outside the UK who want to remain connected to BWY’s professional community.

Membership includes access to:

  • Online training.
  • BWY Member Sessions.
  • Online CPD events.
  • Online CPD and teaching resources.

Overseas Teacher Membership does not include teaching insurance.
