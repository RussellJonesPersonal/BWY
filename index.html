Viewing the site at different screen sizes

Google Chrome can show you how a page will look on a phone, tablet, laptop or larger screen without needing to have each device available.

  1. Open one of the prototype pages in Google Chrome.
  2. Press F12 on your keyboard to open Chrome's developer tools.
  3. At the top of the developer tools, click the small phone and tablet icon.
  4. A menu will appear above the website. Choose a device from the list, such as an iPhone, Pixel or iPad.
  5. You can select different devices to see how the design changes at different screen sizes.
  6. When you have finished, press F12 again to close the developer tools.
